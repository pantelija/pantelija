Collaboration Provenance

Unimatic × Lorenzi Milano

Lorenzi Milano is a Milanese maison established in 1929, renowned for fine accessories, cutlery, and luxury goods. This partnership produced one of the most technically significant references in Unimatic's catalog — marking the brand's first titanium case and a pivotal shift in the Modello Due's construction.

Partner Identity
Lorenzi Milano — Est. 1929
A heritage Milanese retailer specializing in luxury accessories and fine objects. Known for collaborations that emphasize material excellence and Italian craftsmanship. The pairing with Unimatic channels both brands' dedication to refined, understated design.
Design Deviations
What Makes It Unique
Full titanium case construction (a Unimatic first). Deep green colorway across dial and strap. Reduced case thickness from 13.7mm to 11.6mm. Transition from Seiko NH35 to Sellita SW200-1 movement. Drilled lugs for easy strap swaps. Clean, sterile upper dial with all branding below center.

Timeline

Model 2 Evolution

2018
Modello Due Launches
Unimatic introduces the Model 2 field watch platform in stainless steel with Seiko NH35 movement. A larger, more tool-oriented sibling to the Modello Uno.
2018–2022
Collaborations Expand
Multiple limited editions released in steel, building the Model 2's reputation while collectors note consistent feedback on case weight and thickness.
Sep 2022
U2S-T-L — The Titanium Shift
In collaboration with Lorenzi Milano, Unimatic debuts titanium construction. The case drops to 11.6mm thick, sheds 31% of its weight, and introduces the Sellita SW200-1. A watershed moment for the platform.
