Simple setup, powerful impact

Up and running
in minutes

Despite the sophisticated targeting behind it, setting up Zones is remarkably straightforward. No extra hardware, no network reconfiguration — just a few clicks in your Fydelia dashboard.

1

Group your Access Points into Zones

In the Fydelia dashboard, name your zones (e.g. "Main Bar", "Garden Terrace", "Lobby") and assign the relevant APs to each group.

2

Assign a splash page to each Zone

Link an existing splash page to each zone, or create a new one with the WYSIWYG builder. Each zone can have its own design, promotions, and data capture fields.

3

Guests are served the right page automatically

Fydelia detects which AP a guest connects to and serves the corresponding splash page instantly — no guest action required.

Every offer is meaningful
to where the guest is sitting

By promoting the right products at the exact point of consumption, you drive more relevant sales while simultaneously improving the guest experience.

Drive more relevant sales

A bar promotion shown at the bar converts far better than a generic one shown to everyone. Context is everything.

Maintain distinct area identities

A luxury spa and a lively sports bar live on the same network but feel completely different digitally — preserving the unique atmosphere of each sub-space.

Understand how guests move through your space
