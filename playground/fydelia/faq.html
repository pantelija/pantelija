Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Fydelia — from setup and splash pages to integrations, pricing, and compliance.

30 questions answered Fydelia by Sentinel Software Ltd

Getting Started

1. What is Fydelia?

Fydelia is a captive portal and WiFi marketing platform that turns your guest WiFi into a lead-generation and customer loyalty engine. When guests connect to your WiFi, they're greeted with a branded splash page that captures verified contact details and delivers targeted promotions — all automatically.

2. How does the guest WiFi login process work?

When a guest selects your WiFi network, your router redirects them to your Fydelia-powered splash page. The guest authenticates, fills out your form, and accepts your terms. Fydelia then tells the router to grant internet access, and the guest's data flows into your dashboard and any connected platforms in real time.

3. How quickly can I get set up?

You can be live in under 30 minutes. Paste your website URL, our AI builds a branded splash page in seconds, and we remote-configure your router. No on-site visit required — we handle the initial setup for you at no cost.

4. Do I need to install new hardware?

No. Fydelia is a cloud-based portal server — it works with your existing access points and router. No additional boxes, no on-site installations.

5. What hardware is supported?

Fydelia supports 20+ vendors and 40+ device models, including Cisco Meraki, Ubiquiti UniFi, Ruckus, TP-Link Omada, Aruba, EnGenius, MikroTik, DrayTek, SonicWall, Zyxel Nebula, and many more. If it broadcasts WiFi, chances are Fydelia runs on it. Don't see your hardware? Contact our team and we'll check compatibility, usually within a few hours.

Splash Pages & Design

6. How do I create a splash page?

You have two options. Our AI builder lets you paste your website URL — it extracts your logo, brand colours, photos, and copy to generate a fully working splash page in seconds. Or, use the drag-and-drop WYSIWYG editor for full creative control with pre-built sections, real-time mobile preview, and custom CSS support.

7. Can I create multiple splash pages?

Yes — every plan includes unlimited splash pages. Create as many as you need for different venues, promotions, or campaigns. All pages are mobile-responsive and load instantly.

8. What are Splash Page Zones?

Zones let you serve a different splash page to different areas of your venue based on which access point a guest connects to — all on a single SSID. For example, show a cocktail promotion at the bar and a spa deal at the gym, managed from one dashboard.

Features & Marketing Tools

9. What are AdCards?

AdCards are customisable digital cards displayed on your splash page that showcase promotions, events, or brand messages. They transform the opt-in moment from a vague permission request into a compelling value exchange — dramatically increasing consent rates.

10. What are Flash Offers and Dweller Flash Offers?

Flash Offers are time-limited deals displayed the moment a guest connects, complete with countdown timers to drive urgency. Dweller Flash Offers go a step further — they target guests who are already connected to your WiFi, pushing promotions directly to their screens while they're still in your venue.

11. How does gamification work?

Fydelia offers digital scratch cards, spin-to-win wheels, and other interactive games built into the WiFi login experience. You control the probability-weighted prizes and can tune them live. Unique voucher codes sync to your POS, and anti-fraud limits prevent abuse.

12. What is Scheduled Content?

You can schedule different promotions, images, and messages to display based on time of day, day of week, or specific date ranges. Your morning coffee deal shows at breakfast and automatically switches to happy hour in the evening — without manual intervention.

13. Can I run A/B tests?

Yes. Test different headlines, images, and offers side by side. Fydelia lets real-world data decide which version drives the highest engagement and opt-in rates.

14. How do Birthday Campaigns work?

Capture guest birthdays during the WiFi sign-in process, then automatically send personalised celebration offers on their birthday — no manual effort required.

15. Can Fydelia help me get more Google and TripAdvisor reviews?

Yes. Fydelia can automatically prompt guests to leave reviews on your preferred platform after they connect. You can route happy guests to public review sites and privately capture feedback from the rest.

Data, Integrations & CRM

16. What guest data does Fydelia capture?

Fydelia captures verified email addresses, phone numbers, names, birthdays, and any other fields you add to your splash page form. All emails are 100% auto-verified before granting WiFi access.

17. What integrations are available?

Over 100 no-code integrations, including Mailchimp, HubSpot, Salesforce, Klaviyo, Stripe, Shopify, OpenTable, SevenRooms, Google Analytics, TripAdvisor, Zapier, and many more. Two-way data sync means your CRM, email, POS, and analytics tools stay up to date automatically.

18. How do integrations work?

Browse the integration library in your dashboard, select your platform, and authorise the connection with a single click — no API keys or code. From that moment, every guest opt-in, visit, and interaction is automatically piped to your connected platforms in real time.

19. Can I export my data?

Yes. You can export your guest lists as CSV files at any time for further analysis or use in external tools.

Multi-Venue & Enterprise

20. How does Shared Sign-On work?

Once a guest signs in at any venue in your estate, their device is securely remembered account-wide. Every subsequent visit at any of your locations is instant — no splash page, no form, no friction.

21. What is Proof of Presence?

Even when guests auto-connect without seeing a sign-in page, Fydelia logs every visit as a Proof of Presence event. This data is sent to your CRM in real time, so you can track repeat visits, measure footfall, and understand loyalty across your entire portfolio — with zero data gaps.

22. Can I manage all my venues from one dashboard?

Yes. Fydelia's Full Remote Management gives you centralised control over every venue. Update branding, launch campaigns, deploy splash pages, and monitor network health across all locations — from anywhere, with no site visits required.

Security, Privacy & Compliance

23. Is Fydelia GDPR compliant?

Yes, fully. Fydelia is engineered for GDPR compliance from the ground up. Login forms never use pre-ticked boxes — every opt-in is an explicit, affirmative choice. Granular micro opt-ins let guests consent to specific communications independently. A verifiable audit trail is created automatically for every consent event.

24. How is guest data protected?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols. Access is restricted to authorised personnel only with multi-factor authentication. Fydelia's servers are hosted in data centres with ISO 27001 and SOC 2 certifications. Regular penetration testing and vulnerability scans are conducted.

25. What is the data retention policy?

Fydelia retains guest data for a standard period of one year from the date of collection. Custom retention periods can be requested in writing. At the end of the retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

26. What happens if there's a data breach?

Fydelia maintains an Incident Response Plan and commits to notifying affected customers within 24 hours of breach detection, with full details on the scope, impact, and remediation steps taken.

Pricing & Billing

27. How much does Fydelia cost?

Pricing is low, per-venue, and every feature is included — no tiers, no upsells. Food & Beverage venues start from £35/mo (≈ $45 / €40), Entertainment from £45/mo (≈ $55 / €50), and Hospitality from £65/mo (≈ $80 / €75). Discounts are available based on billing cycle and number of venues.

28. Is there a free trial?

Yes — a completely free 14-day trial with access to every single feature. No credit card required, no restrictions, and we handle the setup for you.

29. Am I locked into a long-term contract?

No. Fydelia operates on a flexible "Pay & Play" commitment — you're only committed for your current billing cycle, typically a rolling 3-month period. Cancel anytime.

30. I'm stuck in a contract with another provider. Can I still switch?

Yes. Fydelia is free until your current contract ends. Switch today and get full service at zero cost for the entire remainder of your existing contract — no double payments. Terms and conditions apply; contact the team for details.
