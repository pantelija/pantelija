The Journey

From three providers to
one simple solution

Awen have a wide range of needs, from small venues to large outdoor spaces. We asked how we're doing.

1

The Challenge

What was a challenge before using Fydelia?

We were previously using three different guest WiFi solutions, each with its own idiosyncrasies and higher costs. When our O2 WiFi contract in the Costa Coffee stores was ceased, we needed an immediate turnkey solution that could cover the full range of our venues.

2

Getting Started

How did you find the process of getting started with Fydelia?

From the start, the Fydelia team were friendly and willing to offer solutions tailored to our needs, without any hard sell. The onboarding was smooth and straightforward. We felt supported throughout the entire process of bringing all ten venues onto a single platform.

3

The Results

What is life like now?

Much easier, with only one guest WiFi solution to manage. Having a single platform for all ten venues means less admin overhead, lower costs, and a consistent experience for visitors whether they're at Maesteg Town Hall or Y Nyth. The team continues to be responsive and helpful whenever we need support.
