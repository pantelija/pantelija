Under the hood

How guest WiFi
splash pages work

The captive portal runs on a separate server, not on your access points. APs handle radio connectivity; Fydelia handles everything else.

Guest connects

A visitor selects your WiFi SSID. The access point accepts the connection but no internet traffic flows yet.

Traffic intercepted

The router/firewall intercepts the HTTP request and redirects the guest's browser to your Fydelia splash page.

Splash page served

Fydelia serves your branded portal. The guest authenticates, accepts terms, fills out your form, or enters a voucher code.

Access granted

The router opens internet access for that device's MAC address. Guest data flows into Fydelia and your connected platforms.

The three components

Your splash page infrastructure is made of three pieces. Fydelia replaces the portal server, and everything else stays as it is.

Access Points

Broadcast the SSID and handle radio connectivity. No portal logic runs here as APs don't serve web pages.

Router / Firewall

Handles traffic interception and walled-garden enforcement. Blocks everything except the splash page server until the guest authenticates.

Captive Portal Server (Fydelia)

Serves the actual splash page HTML with all your branding, forms, and engagement features. This is what Fydelia replaces - no additional hardware required.
