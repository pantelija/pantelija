Proof of Presence

Seamless for guests.
No blind spots for you.

A seamless login for your guests shouldn't mean a gap in your business analytics. Fydelia's Proof of Presence technology works silently in the background — even when guests never see a sign-in page.

Every auto-connection generates a new Proof of Presence event, sent instantly to your CRM or analytics platform. You'll accurately track repeat visits, measure footfall, and understand customer loyalty right across your whole estate.

  • ✓ Every visit logged — even silent auto-connections
  • ✓ Cross-venue customer view in your CRM
  • ✓ Accurate footfall data across your whole portfolio
  • ✓ Link repeat visits to specific campaigns

The smartest way to connect and understand your customers. Shared Sign-On and Proof of Presence work together to offer ultimate convenience for guests while gathering the powerful data you need to grow your business — without compromise.
