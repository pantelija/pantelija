How it works

Three steps to a
seamless return

From first sign-in to fully automatic reconnection — here's the experience your guests and your data team both love.

01

First visit: normal sign-in

On their first visit, the guest signs in through your branded splash page and connects to the WiFi as usual. Their device is securely remembered at this point.

02

Device is securely remembered

Fydelia logs the guest's device identifier against their profile in your CRM. No passwords stored — just a secure, privacy-compliant device fingerprint.

03

Every return: instant reconnection

On every subsequent visit, their device is recognized the moment it pings the network. They're connected instantly — no splash page, no form, no friction.
