The Journey

From no portal to
review-driving WiFi

Josh Catton, Resort Director, walks us through how Seaview went from having no splash page control to a WiFi experience that directly impacts their bottom line.

1

The Challenge

What was life like before Fydelia?

Prior to integration with Fydelia, we were unable to find a means to manage and customise a splash page for our customer-facing WiFi portal. We also needed a solution which offered both free and chargeable access. With Fydelia, the engineers worked with us to build a splash page which was user-friendly, simple to manage, and offered both free and chargeable packages.

2

Getting Started

How was the onboarding experience?

I was regularly consulted by Fydelia's engineers during the integration process to ensure the product being delivered was tailored and meeting all of my requested features. The support team were always very helpful and prompt in response times. It felt like a genuine collaboration rather than an off-the-shelf install.

3

The Results

What is life like now?

Customers demand WiFi on holiday - especially in their own private area following the COVID-19 pandemic. Seeing reviews online about a holiday village's strong WiFi is hugely beneficial for attracting business customers and guests who greatly desire connectivity. It's integral to have a simple, quick and painless sign-up on your splash page. When a customer struggles accessing a network, it creates a bad tone for their entire holiday and many post reviews mentioning WiFi specifically. Having a painless, simple customer-facing splash page backed up by good speeds means the customer is pleased and will post a positive review, leaving a higher rating on our online listings. The team is helpful, responsive and very prompt through set-up and the after-sales period.
