Micro opt-ins

Don't lose a guest
who says "not yet".

A guest who declines your general marketing list isn't lost — they're just waiting for the right ask. AdCards' Micro Opt-ins let you target them with something specific and irresistible.

Present a highly relevant card even to guests who've opted out of everything else: "Want to be notified about our monthly quiz night?" or "Opt in to receive a special birthday offer." One specific value proposition is far easier to say yes to than a blanket marketing list.

  • ✓ Reach guests who declined your main marketing list
  • ✓ Capture interest for specific events, offers, or services
  • ✓ Fully GDPR-compliant — each micro opt-in is a separate consent
  • ✓ Build a wider, more engaged audience over time
